Who we are

cringe. is run by an independent developer based in India, who decides how and why your personal data is used. In legal terms we are the data controller (or Data Fiduciary under Indian law). Contact for all privacy matters: cringe.app.support@gmail.com.

Who can see your data

  • Your partner sees the content you share in your space, your name, your profile photo, your birthday and, if you turn it on, your city and the distance between you.
  • Nobody else in the app. Every table is protected by row-level security rules, so one couple can never read another couple’s data. Photos are stored privately and only opened through short-lived links (1 hour).
  • The app operator. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, but it is not end-to-end encrypted, so the operator could technically access it. We only do so when needed to run the service, to deal with a report of abuse, or when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our instructions, under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Cloudflare (R2): storing photos, doodles and profile pictures.
  • Google: “Continue with Google” sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android notifications.
  • Expo (650 Industries): delivering push notifications.
  • Our email provider: sending account emails such as sign-up confirmation.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or if the app is ever transferred to a new owner (who would have to respect this policy).

International transfers

We are based in India, and our providers run servers in several countries, including the United States, the European Union and India. Your data may therefore be stored or processed outside the country where you live. When data from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, we rely on our providers’ Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) or other safeguards the law allows. Contact us for more details.

Data stored on your phone

To work smoothly, the app keeps a few things on your phone:

  • your login session, encrypted, with its key kept in your phone’s secure keystore;
  • your settings: theme, language, app lock, notification preferences;
  • copies of photos and doodles you’ve viewed, so they load quickly without downloading again.

These stay on your phone only. They are removed when you uninstall the app, and the saved photos are also cleared when you unpair or delete your account. The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS, and stored data is encrypted at rest by our providers.
  • Access to the database is limited by row-level security, and photo links expire after 1 hour.
  • You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or phone PIN to open the app.
  • If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law where you live requires.

How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • When you unpair or delete your account, we delete the related data immediately, including stored photo, doodle and profile picture files.
  • Backups: we currently don’t keep backups, so deleted data is gone for good. If we add backups in future, deleted data would remain in them for at most 7 days and would never be restored into the app.
  • Our providers’ short-lived technical logs expire on their own, typically within days.
  • We may keep a record of a request you made to us (for example, a deletion request) for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and deletion

  • Unpair (Settings → Danger zone) ends your shared space and permanently deletes all its notes, snaps, doodles, dates and streak for both partners.
  • Delete my account (Settings → Danger zone) permanently deletes your account, your profile and your shared space.
  • Can’t access the app? See Delete your account.
  • You can turn off location sharing, notifications and app lock, and change your language, at any time in Settings.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a common, machine-readable format (portability);
  • correct data that is wrong (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete your data (you can also do it yourself in the app);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw consent for optional features at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Email cringe.app.support@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We may need to confirm it’s you. We reply within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows), and we never treat you differently for using your rights. Someone you authorise may also make a request for you, with proof of that authorisation. Below are the extra details for specific countries.
