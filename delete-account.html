Delete your account

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open cringe. and tap the ⚙️ settings button at the top of any screen.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice (and with your fingerprint or PIN if App lock is on).

Want to keep your account but end the shared space? Tap Unpair in the same section instead.

Can't open the app? Ask us by email

Send an email to cringe.app.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my cringe. account”, from the email address you signed up with. We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login details, and your profile (name, profile photo, location, notification token).
  • Your shared space with your partner: all notes, snaps, doodles, captions, important dates and the streak. This deletes them for your partner too.
  • All photo, doodle and profile picture files, permanently erased from storage.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. We don't keep backups of deleted accounts.
